MAHATMA GANDHI NATIONAL RURAL EMPLOYMENT GUARANTEE ACT (MGNREGA)
Nodal Ministry: Department of Rural Development, Ministry of Rural Development, Government of India.

SCHEME OVERVIEW:
MGNREGA is a labor law and social security measure that aims to guarantee the 'right to work'. It provides at least 100 days of guaranteed wage employment in a financial year to every rural household whose adult members volunteer to do unskilled manual work.

BENEFITS:
1. Guarantee of 100 days of unskilled manual work per financial year to registered rural households.
2. Unemployment allowance: If an applicant is not provided employment within 15 days of submitting the application for work, they are entitled to a daily unemployment allowance.
3. Wage payment must be made within 15 days from the date of closure of the muster roll. If wages are delayed beyond 15 days, workers are entitled to compensation at the rate of 0.05% of unpaid wages per day of delay.
4. Work site facilities: Drinking water, shade, first-aid box, and creche (if more than 5 children under age 6 are present).
5. Work site must be located within 5 km of the applicant's residence. If it is further than 5 km, an extra 10% wage is payable to meet travel cost.

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
1. Must be a citizen of India residing in a rural area.
2. Adult members of the household must volunteer to perform unskilled manual labor.
3. The household must apply for a Job Card to the local Gram Panchayat.
4. All adult members registered under a single Job Card are eligible to work, up to a collective household limit of 100 days per year.

APPLICATION & JOB CARD PROCESS:
1. Registration is made with the Gram Panchayat. The Job Card must be issued within 14 days of registration free of cost.
2. An individual has a right to submit a written application for work, stating the period and timing of work. The Gram Panchayat must issue a dated receipt for this work request.

RTI & COMPLAINTS FOR WAGE DELAYS:
Delays in wage payments, non-issuance of Job Cards, or failure to pay the unemployment allowance are rampant violations. Citizens have a legal right to request records, muster rolls, and payment slips. The RTI application should be addressed to the State Public Information Officer (SPIO) or Block Development Officer (BDO) at the local Block/Panchayat level, or to the CPIO, Ministry of Rural Development, Krishi Bhawan, New Delhi.
Common queries under RTI for MGNREGA include:
- Reasons for the delay of wage payments for work completed in specific muster rolls.
- Detail of the calculation of delay compensation owed to the worker.
- Official registers of employment demanded and employment allocated for the financial year.
- Copy of the measurement book and wage lists for completed works.
