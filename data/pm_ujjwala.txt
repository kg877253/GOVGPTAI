PRADHAN MANTRI UJJWALA YOJANA (PMUY) — LPG GAS CONNECTION
Nodal Ministry: Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas, Government of India.
Implementing Agencies: Indian Oil Corporation (IOCL), Bharat Petroleum (BPCL), Hindustan Petroleum (HPCL).

SCHEME OVERVIEW:
PMUY aims to provide free LPG cooking gas connections to women belonging to Below Poverty Line (BPL) families, primarily targeting rural households where cooking on firewood, cow dung, and kerosene poses severe health risks to women and children.

BENEFITS:
1. Free LPG connection with security deposit waiver (usually Rs. 1,600–2,000 deposited by Government).
2. One free initial gas cylinder refill provided at the time of connection.
3. 14.2 kg cylinder or 5 kg cylinders provided based on applicant's choice.
4. Under PMUY 2.0 (2021): Migrants/women without proof of permanent residence can also apply using a self-declaration.
5. First refill and hot plate (stove) can be obtained on EMI basis adjusted against future subsidy.

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
1. Must be an adult woman of 18 years or above.
2. Must belong to BPL (Below Poverty Line) household (SECC-2011 or other state BPL lists).
3. Priority categories include: SC/ST families, PM Awas Yojana (Gramin and Urban) beneficiaries, Antyodaya Anna Yojana (AAY) families, Forest Dwellers, PMAY-G beneficiaries, Tea and ex-Tea Garden Tribes (Assam), People in riverine Islands.
4. No existing LPG connection must exist in the household under any family member's name.
5. Must have an Aadhaar card and a bank account (Jan Dhan or regular).

EXCLUSIONS:
1. Any household already having an LPG connection registered under ANY family member's name is NOT eligible.
2. Households not appearing in BPL/SECC list and not belonging to any priority category.
3. Households where annual income exceeds the poverty threshold.

APPLICATION PROCESS:
1. The applicant (woman of household) must visit the nearest LPG distributor office.
2. Submit: KYC Form (Form 1 - customer data form), Aadhaar card copy, Bank passbook copy, BPL ration card / self-declaration under PMUY 2.0.
3. Distributor verifies eligibility, submits to oil company's portal.
4. Connection is released within 10-14 working days of approval.

RTI & COMPLAINTS FOR CONNECTION DELAY:
If a qualified applicant's connection is unreasonably delayed, withheld, or rejected without a written communication, she can file an RTI. The application should be sent to:
CPIO, Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas, Shastri Bhawan, Dr. Rajendra Prasad Road, New Delhi – 110001
OR to the Regional Manager / Area Manager of the respective oil PSU (IOCL/BPCL/HPCL).
Common queries under RTI for PMUY include:
- Current status and approval/rejection date of PMUY application submitted on [date] at [distributor name].
- Name and designation of the officer who processed/rejected the application, and a copy of the rejection order.
- Provide the copy of the departmental circular specifying the processing timeline for PMUY connections.
- Provide the register of PMUY applications received by distributor [name] during [month/year], with approval/rejection status.
