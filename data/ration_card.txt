NATIONAL FOOD SECURITY ACT (NFSA) - RATION CARD & FOOD GRAIN DISTRIBUTION
Nodal Ministry: Department of Food and Public Distribution, Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food and Public Distribution, Government of India.

SCHEME OVERVIEW:
Under the NFSA 2013, up to 75% of the rural population and 50% of the urban population are covered for receiving subsidized food grains under the Targeted Public Distribution System (TPDS).

BENEFITS & SCHEME CATEGORIES:
1. Antyodaya Anna Yojana (AAY) Households:
   - Covers the poorest of the poor households.
   - Entitled to receive 35 kg of food grains per family per month.
   - Prices: Rice at Rs. 3/kg, Wheat at Rs. 2/kg, Coarse grains at Rs. 1/kg (Currently made free under the Pradhan Mantri Garib Kalyan Anna Yojana - PMGKAY).
2. Priority Households (PHH):
   - Households identified by states based on specific exclusion/inclusion criteria.
   - Entitled to receive 5 kg of food grains per person per month.
   - Same subsidized rates apply (currently free under PMGKAY).

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
1. Citizens must apply for a Ration Card in their respective State Food & Civil Supplies Department.
2. Rural and urban coverage parameters are determined by the Planning Commission based on Census data, but state governments determine the specific household selection criteria.
3. Priority categories typically include landless laborers, marginal farmers, rural artisans, daily wage earners, widows, terminally ill persons, disabled persons, and female-headed households.

EXCLUSIONS (Varies slightly by state, typical parameters):
1. Any member of the family is a government employee (regular, state or central).
2. Family owns a four-wheeler.
3. Family owns land exceeding specific acreage (e.g. 5 acres in most states).
4. Monthly income of the household exceeds state thresholds (typically Rs. 10,000 to Rs. 15,000).
5. Paying Income Tax.
6. Owning commercial or residential space above a certain size in urban areas.

RTI & COMPLAINTS FOR CORRUPTION / DELAY:
Ration distribution is prone to dealer corruption, short-measuring, non-issue of cards, or illegal cancellation. Under Section 6 of the RTI Act 2005, citizens can request details from the Assistant Commissioner (Food & Civil Supplies) or District Supply Officer (DSO) at the district level, or the Block Ration Officer.
Common queries under RTI for Ration Cards include:
- Reasons for delay in issuing a new Ration Card or adding a family member.
- Daily sale registers and stock register copies of the specific Fair Price Shop (FPS) dealer.
- Visual inspection reports of the FPS shop by the supply inspector.
- Details of grain allocated vs grain distributed by the FPS dealer for specific months.
