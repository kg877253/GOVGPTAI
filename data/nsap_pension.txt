NATIONAL SOCIAL ASSISTANCE PROGRAMME (NSAP) — OLD AGE PENSION, WIDOW PENSION, DISABILITY PENSION
Nodal Ministry: Ministry of Rural Development, Government of India.

SCHEME OVERVIEW:
NSAP is a centrally-sponsored scheme that provides financial assistance to the elderly, widows, and persons with disabilities belonging to Below Poverty Line (BPL) households. It is implemented through four components.

FOUR SUB-SCHEMES:
1. INDIRA GANDHI NATIONAL OLD AGE PENSION SCHEME (IGNOAPS):
   - For persons aged 60 years and above belonging to BPL households.
   - Pension: Rs. 200/month for 60-79 years; Rs. 500/month for 80 years and above.
   - State governments typically add additional amounts on top of the central share.

2. INDIRA GANDHI NATIONAL WIDOW PENSION SCHEME (IGNWPS):
   - For widows aged 40-79 years belonging to BPL households.
   - Pension: Rs. 300/month (central share).

3. INDIRA GANDHI NATIONAL DISABILITY PENSION SCHEME (IGNDPS):
   - For persons aged 18-79 years with severe/multiple disabilities belonging to BPL households.
   - Pension: Rs. 300/month (central share).

4. NATIONAL FAMILY BENEFIT SCHEME (NFBS):
   - Lump sum assistance of Rs. 20,000 to BPL households on death of primary breadwinner aged 18-59.

ELIGIBILITY:
1. Must belong to a BPL household as defined by the State Government.
2. Age proof and Disability Certificate (for disability pension) required.
3. Death Certificate of husband required for widow pension.
4. Must not be receiving any other pension from Central or State Government above Rs. 2,000/month.

APPLICATION PROCESS:
1. Application submitted to Gram Panchayat (rural) or Urban Local Body (urban).
2. Panchayat forwards to Block Development Officer (BDO) / Child Development Project Officer.
3. State Government approves and adds to DBT (Direct Benefit Transfer) list.
4. Pension credited directly to Aadhaar-linked bank account.

RTI & COMPLAINTS FOR PENSION DELAY/STOPPAGE:
Many beneficiaries find their pensions stopped without notice, or new applications stuck indefinitely. RTI is the fastest way to demand answers.
The application should be sent to: SPIO, Block Development Officer (BDO), [Block Name, District] OR CPIO, Ministry of Rural Development, Krishi Bhawan, New Delhi – 110001.
Common queries under RTI for NSAP:
- Provide the date on which pension under IGNOAPS/IGNWPS was sanctioned and the date from which payments began for applicant [Name], [Village].
- Provide the reason in writing with supporting file notes for the stoppage of pension payment since [Month/Year].
- Provide a copy of the official list of NSAP beneficiaries approved for [Village/Ward] during [Financial Year].
- Provide the name and designation of the officer responsible for processing fresh NSAP applications in [Block].
- Provide the number of pending NSAP applications in [Block] as of [Date].
